Open Tallimo from its private link

Tallimo opens only from the private link it makes when it starts. You don't type it in, and there's no password to remember.

To open it, run this in the Tallimo folder:

./rollup-api open

Without the link, Tallimo won't show your updates. That keeps other web pages, and other accounts on this Mac, from opening them.
